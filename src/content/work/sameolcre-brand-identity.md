## The brief

Build an identity for sameolcre® that works across creative direction, media and culture work — something that reads as a studio, not a logo.

## The system

The wordmark is set in all-caps IBM Plex Mono. The C and R are replaced by © and ®, drawn as a matched pair so the name reads as one continuous line: **SAMEOL©®E**.

Around it sits a family of illustrated marks — the paper wasp in side profile as the signature, a symmetrical top view for badges and seals, the nest for anything about process, and a simple hive icon for small sizes.

The palette is deliberately short: off-white as the ground, black as the ink, and Wasp Yellow used like a warning label rather than a background. Labels sit in "quotation marks", sections are numbered, and caution tape, corner brackets and slashes frame the work without leading it.

## Deliverables

Wordmark and tagline lockups, four logo marks, an icon set, textures and accents, color and type specifications, and a full export pack in SVG and PNG.
