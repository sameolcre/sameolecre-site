The first pass paired the wasp and nest as the primary mark with a heavy sans wordmark. The second sheet added the top-view wasp, a hive icon and more nest textures, and tightened the lockups.

From there the wordmark moved to IBM Plex Mono in all caps, with © and ® replacing the C and R. The finished system is in [the case study](/work/sameolcre-brand-identity/).
